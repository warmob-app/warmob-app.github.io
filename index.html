WarMOB

An independent iPhone and iPad app for Warcon, the open-source RCON panel for WARDOGS game servers.

Watch your servers live, see who's playing, and handle players, rotation and moderation from your phone, with the same permissions your account has on your panel. There is no WarMOB account and no WarMOB server: the app connects to your own panel (and the player pictures and sign-in pages it points to), and collects nothing.

What you need

  • A Warcon panel served over HTTPS with a certificate your iPhone trusts (for example Let's Encrypt or Cloudflare). Plain http:// addresses aren't supported.
  • The panel reachable from your phone: on the internet, or through a VPN.
  • No extra login page in front of the panel (such as Cloudflare Access or Authelia).
  • An account on that panel. Sign in with your username and password (and two-factor code), or with Discord or Steam through your panel's own sign-in page.
